Explanations we can test

“Challenged” rejects a particular explanation, not the experimental observation.

1. Observed coordinates → computed contact candidates

Identical Changed Unresolved · Cells are sequence-derived candidates, not observed target binding sites. Select a cell for its evidence. A backbone contact does not make a substitution harmless.

Exact contact atoms and source context

2. Apo predictions are a separate comparison

Sequence pairing comes first. The global CA fit uses all available consensus pairs with target pLDDT ≥70, without outlier trimming. No experimental ion is transferred into a predicted model.

Large residuals can reflect correspondence error, conformational differences, or model error. Even NTCP's own apo prediction need not match its substrate-bound conformation. pLDDT is local confidence, not certainty in the alignment between proteins.

3. What did the experiments measure?

Binding, uptake, inhibition, and infection are separate outcomes. Conflicting studies retain their own contexts.

Substitutions use reference-sequence coordinates, not a claim that the tested construct is wild type. A detected reduction in uptake is not a statement that all uptake is absent.

4. Small, source-backed mechanisms

Physical interaction edges describe binding. Separate drug-inhibition and substitution edges describe measured functional consequences.

Reuse an explanation without losing its limits

Use a stable assertion ID together with the bundle version and SHA-256 in the export manifest. Every claim has an evidence origin, review status, source references, and limitations.
