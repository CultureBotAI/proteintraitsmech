minor / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-2-8-1-dr-curator-provenance.

### F3: Chemical cofactors are typed as protein residues

major / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-2-8-1-cofactor-node-type.

The identified FMN and reduced-FMN molecules are assigned RESIDUE. The schema defines that node type as a specific residue or residue set on a protein, whereas LIGAND includes cofactors. Rhea reactivePart and the valid part_of relations do not convert these chemical cofactors into protein residues. This finding is independent of the balanced reaction and makes no amino-acid-only or sequence-position assumption.

### F4: Explicitly ambiguous names are stored as exact synonyms

minor / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-2-8-1-ambiguous-exact-aliases.

The target stores pyruvate oxidoreductase and pyruvate synthetase as EXACT_SYNONYM, while the current IUBMB page explicitly marks both ambiguous. This authority qualification supports narrowing their scope without disputing the stable EC identity or the remaining alternative.
