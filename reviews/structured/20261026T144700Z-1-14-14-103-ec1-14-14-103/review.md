major / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-14-14-103-automatic-reviewed-promotion.

The maintained graph builder implements that automatic transition, whereas REVIEWED denotes curator sign-off on label, definition and parents. The observed route does not establish that sign-off. ### F2: Flavin cofactors are typed as sequence residues

major / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-14-14-103-flavin-node-type.

The sub2_rp1 and prd2_rp1 nodes are grounded to CHEBI:57618 (FMNH2) and CHEBI:58210 (FMN), but both have node_type RESIDUE. Current ChEBI identifies these molecular entities as cofactors, and the schema reserves RESIDUE for a specific residue or residue set while LIGAND explicitly includes small-molecule cofactors. Rhea's reactivePart relation identifies the reacting group in the generic carrier; it does not make that group an amino-acid residue. This is a bounded node-category error: the chemical identities, generic carriers, part_of edges and lack of sequence-coordinate claims are preserved.

### F3: A gene designation is stored as an exact class synonym

minor / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-14-14-103-gene-alias-scope.

CYP71D12 is stored as EXACT_SYNONYM of tabersonine 16-hydroxylase. IUBMB labels it a gene name, and the current P98183/CYP71D12 and U5HKE8/CYP71D351 annotations plus PMID 24108213 demonstrate two distinct genes for this activity. The source alias remains useful, but exact class equivalence is too broad.
