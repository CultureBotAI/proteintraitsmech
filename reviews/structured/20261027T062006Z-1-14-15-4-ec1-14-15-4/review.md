### F2: Iron-sulfur cofactors are typed as amino-acid residues

major / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-14-15-4-iron-sulfur-reactive-part-type.

The erroneous internal space is a normalization defect; the reaction identity remains supported.

### F4: Two legacy aliases need explicit relation scope

minor / open / confirmed; issue key: ec-1-14-15-4-overexact-legacy-alias-scope.
