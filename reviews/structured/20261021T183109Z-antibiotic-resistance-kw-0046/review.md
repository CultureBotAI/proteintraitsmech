## Findings

### F1: Antibiotic resistance is routed away from its dedicated category

major / open / confirmed; issue key: uniprot-keyword.KW-0046.resistance-category.

The exact defining claim is a protein conferring antimicrobial resistance, while FUNC_RESISTANCE explicitly represents that function and its drug/mechanism scope. _ENV_RE routes the keyword to environmental response based on its name. Generic response grounding does not preserve this more specific representation.

### F2: Broad antibiotic response is asserted as resistance equivalence

major / open / confirmed; issue key: uniprot-keyword.KW-0046.broader-go-equivalence.

The keyword confers bacterial ability to withstand antibiotics. GO:0046677 instead covers any antibiotic-triggered cell/organism change and includes susceptibility as well as resistance. The direct source mapping is real, but encoding it in true-equivalence xrefs overstates its scope. This finding does not invalidate GO biological-process grounding generally.
