# Causal graphs — Round 93: a pump with no stated energetics

Rounds 67 and 69 curated SMR and MATE, and both were notable for what their definitions
supplied: **the coupling**. EmrE *"couples the efflux … with the import of protons"*; MATE
*"utilize the cationic gradient"*.

Tap's record says only:

> *"Mutations in the Rv1258c (Tap) **efflux pump** **contributing to** antibiotic
> resistance."*

It names the pump and nothing else. So the graph has **no coupling node**, and a test bans
*proton*, *gradient*, *ATP* and *antiport* from its labels — the mistake round 67's own
report warned about for RND/MFS/ABC pumps, guarded here rather than left to judgement.

## Two smaller choices from the same sentence

* **`participates in`, not `enables`.** These records are *mutants* of the pump, and CARD
  says they contribute to resistance without saying the mutation increases transport.
* **The hedge is quoted.** *"Contributing to"* is weaker than the *"confers"* most families
  use, and the note says so.

## whiB7 read and left

No mechanism, no functional name, no pathway — **thinner than round 66's EF-Tu**, which at
least identified an elongation factor. Left as drafts.
