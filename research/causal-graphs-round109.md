# Causal graphs — Round 109: a 16th mechanism kind, and a record with no resistance claim

Two records at opposite ends of the evidence range, in the same round.

## uhpA — the drug is not pumped out, it is not let in

> *"uhpA is a **positive activator** of the fosfomycin **importer** uhpT, **thus** mutations
> to uhpA confer fosfomycin resistance **by reducing uhpT expression**."*

The complete chain in one sentence, with **every causal word CARD's own** — *"positive
activator"*, *"thus"*, *"by reducing"*. That is rare here: most definitions give a role and
a resistance claim with nothing between, which is why rounds 66, 81, 83, 106 and 108 all
had to stop early.

**A 16th mechanism kind.** Every efflux config (rounds 67–79, 93) exports the drug. This
loses a *transporter's activator*, so less drug enters — and it is also not round 71's
resistance-by-absence, where what is lost is the determinant's own catalytic function.
Here it is a **regulatory effect on an importer**.

A test bans *efflux*, *extrud*, *export* and *antiport* from its labels, so the two shapes
cannot borrow each other's vocabulary.

## mshB — a named reaction and no resistance claim at all

> *"mshB is a deacetylase … involved in the second step of mycothiol synthesis. **GlcNAc-Ins
> is deacetylated by MshB to produce GlcN-Ins.**"*

Substrate, product and pathway step — **more chemistry than most records in this corpus** —
and CARD never mentions a drug, a mutation, or resistance. Less than round 95's aftA, which
at least called its product essential.

Its neighbour **mshA** (round 95) says *"inability for antibiotic to ACTIVATE"*; this one
says nothing of the kind. Two records in one pathway, one curatable as a mechanism and one
only as chemistry.
