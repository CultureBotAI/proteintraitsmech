# Causal graphs — Round 106: the record the refused mechanism actually belonged to

Round 83 curated **rpoC** and refused it rifampicin's mechanism, noting: *"rifampicin binds
**rpoB**, not rpoC — so the obvious guess would have been confidently incorrect, not merely
uncited."*

This is rpoB. **CARD does not state that mechanism here either.**

> *"RNA polymerase is a multisubunit enzyme that is necessary for transcription. The
> **beta-subunit** of RNA polymerase forms the active center of the enzyme and
> template/transcript binding sites. Mutations in rpoB gene **confers antibiotic
> resistance**."*

Structurally identical to rpoC's: the polymerase's role, the subunit's structural
contribution, and a bare resistance claim with nothing between. So rpoB gets the same graph
its neighbour got, and **the round 83 refusal turns out to have been doubly right** — the
mechanism was on the wrong subunit *and* not citable on the right one.

## The most inviting uncited edge in the corpus

Rifampicin–rpoB is textbook, and this is the record it belongs to. A test asserts **both**
rpoB's and rpoC's configs contain no *rifampicin*, *rifamycin*, *binds the drug* or
*inhibit* — so neither can acquire it later from memory.

That test is the strongest form of this session's recurring rule: **the claim most likely to
arrive uncited is the one you know best about the record it fits.**
